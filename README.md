# LeadBridge

**A structured B2B marketplace for discovering and managing business opportunities, services, leads, and partnerships.**

### An ALEMZAI Product

> This repository is the official public product showcase for LeadBridge. Production source code, security implementation, database architecture, internal workflows, and proprietary business logic are intentionally kept private.

## Overview

LeadBridge is being developed by **ALEMZAI** to give businesses a more structured way to discover, publish, evaluate, save, and manage commercial opportunities.

Business opportunities are often fragmented across social platforms, messaging apps, spreadsheets, private groups, and personal networks. LeadBridge is designed to bring those activities into a focused marketplace experience.

## Selected Product Capabilities

- Business opportunity and provider listings
- Business and user profiles
- Structured discovery and filtering
- Saved opportunities
- Reporting and moderation workflows
- User dashboards
- Protected account experiences
